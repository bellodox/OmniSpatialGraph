Current human instruction supersedes the historical Carbon Memory workflow: **only OSG is operational memory** for recent tasks, debugging discoveries, code-linked rationale and session handoffs. The wiki is human-facing derived documentation, not operational memory, approval or competing product authority. The frozen [specification](../OSG_MVP_SPEC.md:17) remains normative beneath current human instruction.

Human-authorized cleanup may remove genuinely redundant derived material after transferring unique facts and repairing references; the documents above remain useful independently of the index.

## Excluded procedures

Do not read/update/init a legacy bank, query NotebookLM, generate a documentation graph, refresh external indexes or change hooks/ignore settings for memory maintenance. Do not automatically migrate, initialize, restore or fix doctor caches on error. Explicit human authorization and stopped-writer safeguards govern maintenance; see [recovery](workflows.md#recovery). Formal G5/Q/gate approval is not implied by experimental memory use.
