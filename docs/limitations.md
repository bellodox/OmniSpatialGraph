## Human decisions

- **Licensing and distribution:** The private [manifest](../package.json:4), [LICENSE](../LICENSE) and [NOTICE](../NOTICE) declare Apache-2.0; the frozen [specification](OSG_MVP_SPEC.md:46) describes MIT. Human reconciliation is unresolved. Review third-party grammar provenance/licenses and LanceDB/Sharp/libvips native/LGPL notices and redistribution obligations before distribution. Bundle licensing does not settle individual grammar obligations. No public publishing or agent legal approval.
- **Dependency compatibility:** LanceDB's optional Transformers 3.0.2 requires Sharp ^0.33.5, excluding bundled 0.35.5; the recorded dependency-tree inspection reports ELSPROBLEMS. The supplied 2026-10-15 lockfile audit reports zero advisories, not compatibility. Images remain unsupported; the separate optional embedding adapter is off by default. Its presence does not resolve compatibility or grant risk approval. Owner disposition is still required before enablement or distribution; the original experiment requested disposition by **2026-10-21**. Earlier version-specific High findings are historical, not today's reported audit state.
- **Formal acceptance:** [Q1–Q9](OSG_MVP_SPEC.md:153) and [G0–G6](OSG_MVP_SPEC.md:762) remain human-only. Experiment authorization and bounded reviews are not approval of upstream copying, a new emitter format, commercial privacy review or gate completion. The entire specification remains frozen.

## Separately authorized follow-ups

- **Swift failure preservation:** Source inspection indicates child failure can produce a line fallback with no Swift declarations; sync may retract symbol facts and associated episode links. A later parse may restore symbols without those links. This is a nonblocking inspected risk, **not executed end-to-end loss/corruption proof**. Investigate retaining previous facts or failing sync explicitly, and add a forced-child-failure regression. Earlier manual injection proves isolation only. The persistent child has a 60-second per-request timeout, not a whole-scan timeout; that failure path is unforced and the former 16 MiB output cap is absent.
- **Indexing coverage:** The 2026-10-15 migration and its validation syncs each skipped ambiguous declarations with **742 warnings**; later cleanup syncs each reported **741**. These are dated snapshots, not a fixed current count. Inspect relevant source, report gaps and avoid exhaustive-index claims or automatic fixes. Fresh citations do not refresh historical episode anchors. Explicit rename-chain/return-to-prior-content coverage remains incomplete; graph no-op is not unchanged-file parse-skipping proof. Current reparsing is intentional.
- **Performance and optional vectors:** Measure source-access policy work, committed-row reconstruction/reparsing, persistent-child Swift scans and large-store behavior before further optimizing. Synthetic Swift timing is not a real-repository benchmark. Embedding tests use a fake model; real-model download/inference, dependency-version compatibility and useful real-task recall remain unvalidated. Vector coverage is not quality or trust certification. No trusted-cache/source fast path or performance claim is authorized by passing correctness checks.
- **Platforms and durability:** Linux/macOS, non-Windows case-insensitive filesystems, hostile TOCTOU, network filesystems, forced termination/power loss and abrupt-init staging cleanup are unverified. Headless-table and native-load/sharing-failure tests are modeled/injected. Local table rename/config hard-link behavior is not portable fsync/power-loss proof. Retain the passing CAS access-denied version-hint warning as a diagnostic observation.
- **Current package proof:** Historical install/archive parity does not cover later source/docs. A separately authorized refresh and independent clean install/current parity are needed before making those claims. Native bundles are build-platform-specific; seven optional-platform declarations are not seven execution proofs.
- **Privacy:** Sync/supersession do not purge append-only history. Use a fresh disposable workspace/store for affected pre-fix data; redact diagnostics before sharing. The public worker boundary does not cover all APIs or hostile pre-launch Node configuration; its IPC cap applies after deserialization, not before allocation.
